Credit Card Default Prediction

A logistic-regression classification project connecting statistical concepts with applied financial-risk prediction.

METHODLogistic Regression
DOMAINFinancial Risk
TOOLSPython · pandas · scikit-learn
OBJECTIVE

Predict a binary outcome.

The goal was to distinguish default from non-default outcomes and evaluate the model beyond a single accuracy number.

EVALUATION

Accuracy isn't the whole story.

Precision, recall, specificity, and the confusion matrix give a clearer view of classification errors and tradeoffs.
